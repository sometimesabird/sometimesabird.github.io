Social Media Toxicity and Mental Health

What happens to mental health when toxic content is removed from social media?

We randomize a cross-platform browser extension that hides toxic posts and comments for six weeks (N = 664). Contrary to intuition, filtering toxicity worsens mental health: a 0.15σ rise in a GAD-7/PHQ-8 symptom index, with loneliness and a diminished relative moral self-view as candidate channels.

With Francesco Capozza, Kylan Rutherford, and Mateusz Stalinski
