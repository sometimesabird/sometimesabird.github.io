Variations on “by”

Twenty-two takes on the word, one dimension at a time: the face, the punctuation, the colour, the case, the form. The dimensions combine (an italic, two-tone, on a round plate), so pick the parts you like. Try puts a variant in this page's real tab; ← and → step through them, 1–9 and 0 jump to the first ten.

Drawn on a 16-pixel grid in the Gruvbox palette, letters converted to paths so no font is needed. The serif is DejaVu Serif bold, the closest installed match to the site's Georgia in weight; “Times-like” is Liberation Serif. The plate is shared and can be dropped from any variant whose ink reads on both light and dark tabs (the red ones).
