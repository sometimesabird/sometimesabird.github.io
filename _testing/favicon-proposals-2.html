Favicon proposals

Ten candidates to replace the red atom. Each is shown in a mock light and dark tab strip at tab size, at twice tab size, and large. Try puts a candidate in this page's real tab (the title already matches the site), so you can judge it against your actual open tabs; keys 1–9 and 0 do the same.

the current icon, for comparison

All ten are drawn on a 16-pixel grid in the site's Gruvbox palette, so they stay crisp at tab size. The dark plate is shared; any glyph can also be cut out without it. Whichever is chosen gets exported as favicon.ico (16, 32, 48), a favicon.svg, and an Apple touch icon.
